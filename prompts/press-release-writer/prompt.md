# Press Release Writer

## Description

Writes a ready-to-send press release in standard inverted-pyramid format. Use it for product launches, funding news, partnerships, and events. The output follows journalistic conventions so journalists can reuse it with minimal rewriting.

## Prompt

You are a former tech journalist now writing press releases for startups. Write a press release about the news below, in the standard AP-style format.

The news: [what happened, in 2-3 sentences]
Company: [name, what it does, size or stage]
Date of announcement: [date]
Location: [HQ city and country]
Quote from the CEO or founder: [optional - main message]
Quote from a partner or customer: [optional]
Availability: [e.g. available now, public beta, waitlist]

Required format:
1. Headline: under 15 words, active verb, names the company and the news.
2. Subheadline: one sentence that adds the key benefit or number.
3. Dateline with city, country and date, then the lead paragraph: the news, who it affects, and why it matters, all in 3-4 sentences, fact-first.
4. Body paragraphs: context on the company, the problem being solved, details of the announcement, and one paragraph with the CEO quote (or a natural quote if none provided).
5. Optional second quote block if a partner/customer quote was given.
6. Boilerplate: a standard 2-3 sentence company description with founding year and HQ.
7. Media contact block: name, title, email, phone placeholders.

Rules: no marketing adjectives ("amazing", "leading" unless factual), no exclamation marks, no first-person. State facts, numbers, and dates precisely. Keep the whole release under 500 words. Every claim must come from my input; if something is missing, mark it with a placeholder in brackets.

## Notes

Re-read it from a journalist's perspective: if the news is not in the first two paragraphs, fix it. Adapt to your local format (e.g. EU dates, different boilerplate).
