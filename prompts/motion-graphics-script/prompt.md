# Motion Graphics Script

## Description

Creates a frame-by-frame animation script for a motion graphics video, including text reveals, transitions, and timing. Use it for explainer videos, product launches, or social clips produced in After Effects, Motion, or similar tools.

## Prompt

Act as a motion designer and explainer-video scriptwriter. I will give you a product or concept to explain and a target duration of 30 to 60 seconds. Produce an animation script with these parts:

1. A scene table with columns: Time, Visual, Text on screen, Motion (e.g. slide-in, scale-up, wipe), and Sound.
2. A story arc: problem, solution, proof, call to action.
3. Style direction: color palette, typography suggestion, and animation easing (ease-in-out, bounce, elastic).

Example scene row:

| Time | Visual | Text on screen | Motion | Sound |
|------|--------|----------------|--------|-------|
| 0:06-0:12 | Simple line-art house being built from parts | "Build in minutes" | Parts assemble with a springy ease-out | Upbeat pluck |

Keep each scene under 8 seconds. After the table, list the 5 keyframes for each scene (enter, hold, accent, exit) so the motion is easy to keyframe in a timeline. End with a 20-word voice-over line for the full video.

## Notes

- Scenes under 8 seconds keep the pace fast and the file sizes small.
- Reuse one accent color throughout for brand consistency.
