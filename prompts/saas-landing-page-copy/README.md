# SaaS Landing Page Copy

Generates a full SaaS landing page copy from a one-line product description.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Produces a complete landing page draft: hero, problem, solution, features, testimonials, FAQ, and final CTA.
- Structures copy around a proven pain-point to benefit flow.
- Writes in plain, conversion-focused English without hype words.
