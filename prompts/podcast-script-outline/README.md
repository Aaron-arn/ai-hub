# Podcast Script Outline

Builds a structured podcast episode outline with segments, questions, and outro lines.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Structures the episode into acts with timings.
- Writes five open-ended interview questions.
- Includes a sponsorship transition and ad-lib zone.
