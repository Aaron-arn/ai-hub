# Short Story Writer

Develops a complete short story from premise to polished draft in five stages.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Moves through five stages from premise to polish.
- Writes in scenes with dialogue-driven characters.
- Self-reviews and lists the revisions made.
