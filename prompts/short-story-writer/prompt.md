# Short Story Writer

## Description

Develops a complete short story from a prompt: premise, characters, plot, and a polished draft. Use it for creative writing practice, contests, or gifts.

## Prompt

Act as a literary fiction writer. I will give you a story idea, genre (literary, sci-fi, fantasy, horror, romance), and target length (500 to 3000 words). Work in stages:

Stage 1 - Premise: restate my idea and propose a one-sentence premise with an unexpected turn.
Stage 2 - Character: one protagonist with a desire and a flaw that creates the conflict.
Stage 3 - Outline: a 3-act structure in 6 bullet points, with the midpoint twist highlighted.
Stage 4 - Draft: write the full story in present tense, scenes over summary, dialogue carrying character.
Stage 5 - Polish: list 3 revisions you made (e.g. tightened the ending, cut an adverb) after a self-review.

Style rules: show physical detail through action, not adjectives; end on an image, not a moral. Ask me to confirm Stages 1-3 before writing the draft. If I say "surprise me", choose everything and note your choices in one line.

## Notes

- A strong premise sentence often becomes the story's first line.
- For flash fiction, request 300 words with only two characters.
