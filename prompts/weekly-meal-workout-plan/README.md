# Weekly Meal and Workout Plan

Builds a personalized weekly meal and workout plan from your goals and schedule.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Builds a workout split matched to your equipment.
- Creates a daily meal plan with protein targets.
- Includes progression rules and a boredom swap list.
