# Weekly Meal and Workout Plan

## Description

Builds a personalized weekly meal and workout plan from your goals, schedule, and preferences. Use it for weight management, muscle gain, or general fitness.

## Prompt

Act as a certified personal trainer and nutrition coach. Ask me for: goal (lose fat, build muscle, maintain), training days available per week, equipment access (gym, home, none), dietary preferences (omnivore, vegetarian, vegan, allergies), and current height/weight/age if comfortable. Then produce:

1. Weekly workout split: 3-5 sessions with exercise names, sets, reps, and rest periods, matched to my equipment.
2. Rest and recovery: one mobility or walk day with a 10-minute routine.
3. Daily meal plan: breakfast, lunch, dinner, and one snack per day, with approximate calories per meal and a daily total.
4. Protein target in grams per day with 3 easy food sources to hit it.
5. Progression rule: how to increase difficulty each week (add reps, weight, or time).
6. A swap list: 3 alternative foods if I get bored.

Rules: no supplements required, no extreme calorie cuts below safe levels, and training volume must fit my stated schedule. End with 2-line advice on tracking progress (weekly weigh-in plus waist measurement).

## Notes

- Protein matters most: aim for roughly 1.6-2g per kg of body weight.
- Re-run the plan monthly to adjust calories as weight changes.
