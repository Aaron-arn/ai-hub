# Sales Objection Handling Coach

Practices objection handling with realistic sales role-play scenarios.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Simulates a skeptical prospect who pushes back on your chosen objection.
- Gives structured feedback on your responses with alternatives.
- Escalates difficulty with harder versions of the same objection.
