# Investment Risk Review

Reviews investment ideas for risk, diversification, and alignment with your goals.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Scores risk across four dimensions.
- Runs bull, flat, and bear market scenarios.
- Ends with research questions and a verdict.
