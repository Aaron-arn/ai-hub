# Editorial Illustration

Designs a metaphorical editorial illustration prompt with palette and headline space for articles.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Creates a metaphorical illustration concept for any article topic.
- Locks a 3-color palette and textured style for cohesion.
- Provides a second concept and tweak keywords for tone.
