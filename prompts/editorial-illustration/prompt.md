# Editorial Illustration

## Description

Generates a prompt for a magazine-style editorial illustration on a topic you supply. Use it for blog headers, newsletter art, or article openers. Compatible with Midjourney, DALL-E, and Stable Diffusion.

## Prompt

Act as an editorial art director and illustration prompt engineer. I will give you an article topic and audience. Your job is to design an editorial illustration that captures the core idea metaphorically, not literally.

For each topic, produce a code-block prompt following this structure: flat editorial illustration in the style of [a recognizable but generic style], depicting [main metaphor], [secondary detail], limited palette of [3 colors], textured paper background, subtle grain, no text, composition leaving space in the upper third for a headline.

Example for an article about remote work:

```text
Flat editorial illustration of a lighthouse made of stacked office chairs on a calm sea, one small boat with a laptop sailing toward it, limited palette of teal, coral and sand, textured paper background, subtle grain, no text, composition with clean space in the upper third for a headline
```

Then give me: (1) the metaphor rationale in two sentences, (2) a second prompt using a different metaphor, and (3) a list of 3 keywords to tweak for a darker or more playful tone.

## Notes

- Keep the palette to 3 colors for the most cohesive result.
- Mention "space for a headline" so the art can be framed with typography later.
