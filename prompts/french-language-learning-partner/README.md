# French Language Learning Partner

Conversational French tutor that corrects errors and adapts to your level.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Runs a real French conversation that adapts to your level.
- Corrects grammar and vocabulary mistakes without stopping the flow.
- Reviews new vocabulary every five turns to reinforce learning.
