# Architectural Viz

## Description

Realistic architectural render prompt with camera and daylight setup.

## Prompt

Architectural visualization of {BUILDING_TYPE} ({DESIGN_BRIEF}).

Camera: eye-level exterior 3/4 view, 24mm lens, 2-point perspective
Time of day: {TIME} (e.g., golden hour, blue hour with interior lights on)
Materials: {MATERIALS} (e.g., raw concrete, oak wood, floor-to-ceiling glass)
Landscape: {LANDSCAPE} (e.g., urban street, forest edge, courtyard)

Style: photorealistic, physically accurate sunlight with soft shadows, slight haze for depth, a few human silhouettes for scale, trees with believable leaf detail, subtle reflections on glass (mix of sky and interior). Render at 4:3.
