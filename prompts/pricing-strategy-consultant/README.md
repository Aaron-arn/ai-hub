# Pricing Strategy Consultant

Develops pricing models and packaging tiers for products and services.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Recommends a pricing model and 2-4 tiers with anchoring logic.
- Provides value-based price points, discount strategy, and guardrails.
- Builds a validation experiment plan with warning signs for failure.
