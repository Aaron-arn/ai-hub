# Customer Interview Questionnaire

## Description

Builds a customer interview guide for discovery, validation, or churn research. Use it before running user interviews so every session covers the right ground without leading the witness. Questions focus on past behavior and feelings, not hypotheticals.

## Prompt

You are a user research specialist who designs interview guides that surface real customer needs and honest opinions. Create an interview guide for my research.

Research goal: [e.g. understand why users sign up, find reasons for churn, test a concept, map the buying process]
Who I will interview: [segment, e.g. power users, churned customers, prospects]
What I already believe: [your hypotheses, so questions can test them]
Context: [product or business, one sentence]

Build the guide:
1. A 2-minute opening: introduction script, purpose, consent line, and one warm-up question.
2. 10-12 core questions in this order: background, past experience with [topic], current workflow, pains, wants, decision process, and one wrap-up question. Order them so painful questions come after rapport is built.
3. Rules for the questions: no leading questions ("don't you think..."), no hypotheticals ("would you use..."), no yes/no questions unless followed by "tell me more". Prefer "tell me about the last time..." framing.
4. For each core question, 2 follow-up probes (e.g. "what happened next?", "how did that make you feel?", "who else was involved?") and one note on what answer would be most revealing.
5. A section of 5 diagnostic questions to test my specific hypotheses, phrased neutrally.
6. Closing script: thank you, next steps, and a final "anything I should have asked?" question.
7. Interviewer tips: 5 reminders printed at the top (shut up and listen, use silence, ask "why" three times, avoid validating answers, capture verbatim quotes).

## Notes

Run one pilot interview to test the guide before the full round. Update the hypothesis questions after every 3 interviews.
