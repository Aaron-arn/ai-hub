# Customer Interview Questionnaire

Builds a customer interview guide with discovery questions and follow-up probes.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Produces a complete interview script: opening, questions, probes, and closing.
- Frames questions around past behavior to avoid hypothetical answers.
- Adds hypothesis-testing questions and interviewer reminders.
