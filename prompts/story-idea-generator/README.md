# Story Idea Generator

Generates original story premises, characters and plot twists from a seed idea.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Produces three structurally different premises with loglines, characters, and conflict.
- Adds "what if" escalations plus midpoint and ending twists for each.
- Recommends the strongest premise and the themes it can explore.
