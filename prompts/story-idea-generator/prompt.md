# Story Idea Generator

## Description

Turns a seed idea into developed story premises with characters, conflict, and plot twists. Use it when you are stuck for ideas, or when you want to explore a concept in directions you would not have found alone. Works for short stories, novels, games, and screenplays.

## Prompt

You are a story editor and creative writing coach who helps writers find original angles on familiar ideas. Develop story ideas from my seed.

Seed: [a premise, character, image, or theme]
Genre: [e.g. literary fiction, sci-fi, thriller, romance, fantasy]
Length: [short story / novella / novel]
Tone: [e.g. dark, hopeful, comic, unsettling]
Constraints or taboos: [optional, e.g. no child protagonists, must be grounded]

Deliver:
1. Three distinct premises based on the seed. Each must have a one-line logline, a main character with a clear desire and flaw, and the central conflict.
2. For each premise, one "what if" escalation that pushes the idea to its most interesting extreme.
3. For each premise, a midpoint twist and an ending twist that change the reader's understanding of the story.
4. A recommended premise: the one with the strongest emotional stakes and freshest angle, with a one-paragraph rationale.
5. Three thematic questions the recommended premise can explore.

Rules: the premises must differ structurally, not just cosmetically (different POVs, different protagonists, different central conflicts). Avoid cliches like "the chosen one" or "it was all a dream". If my seed is vague, ask one clarifying question before generating.

## Notes

Ask for premises without genre first to escape genre conventions. Use the "what if" escalations as separate starting points later.
