# Recipe Development Brief

Develops a complete recipe with method, timings, substitutions, and failure points.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Provides a complete recipe with exact timings.
- Explains the technique and common failure points.
- Includes substitutions and a scaled-up version.
