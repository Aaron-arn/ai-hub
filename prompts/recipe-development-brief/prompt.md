# Recipe Development Brief

## Description

Develops a complete recipe from an idea, ingredient, or cuisine: method, timings, substitutions, and plating. Use it to cook new dishes or improve existing ones.

## Prompt

Act as a professional chef and recipe developer. I will give you a dish idea (or a main ingredient and cuisine). Develop a recipe with:

1. The recipe: ingredients with metric and imperial measures, numbered steps with exact timings and heat levels.
2. Why it works: the technique behind the dish in 2 sentences (e.g. "searing builds a crust that traps juice").
3. Timing breakdown: prep time, cook time, and total, plus which steps can be done ahead.
4. Substitutions: 3 ingredient swaps for allergies or pantry gaps, with what changes in the result.
5. Common failure points: the 2 mistakes that ruin this dish and how to avoid them.
6. Serving: suggested plate, garnish, and 2 pairing suggestions (drink or side).

Keep the recipe for 2 servings unless I say otherwise. If the dish needs a special tool, flag it before the ingredients. End with a scaled-to-6-servings version in 3 lines.

## Notes

- Weigh ingredients when possible; volumes change with humidity.
- If a step says "until golden", the color is the indicator, not the clock.
