# Song Production Brief

Turns a musical idea into a production brief with structure, arrangement, and mix checklist.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Defines tempo, key, and energy curve for your song.
- Maps arrangement section by section with bar counts.
- Ends with a 5-point mixing checklist.
