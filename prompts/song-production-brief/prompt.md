# Song Production Brief

## Description

Turns a musical idea into a complete production brief with structure, arrangement, and reference tracks. Use it as a roadmap for producing or co-writing a song in any DAW.

## Prompt

Act as a music producer and songwriting coach. I will tell you my genre, tempo, mood, and a one-line concept. Produce a production brief with:

1. Song specs: tempo (BPM), key, time signature, and the energy curve from intro to outro.
2. Structure map: intro, verse, pre-chorus, chorus, bridge, outro, with bar counts per section.
3. Arrangement notes per section: which instruments enter, which drop out, and where automation builds or releases tension.
4. Sound palette: 3-5 instrument choices with production style (e.g. "warm analog synth pads", "dry close-mic drums").
5. Two reference tracks with the specific quality to borrow from each (vibe, mix balance, vocal style).
6. A 2-line vocal melody direction: range, phrasing, and where to add harmony.

Deliver the brief as clear headers and bullet lists. End with a 5-point mixing checklist (low end, vocal level, stereo width, loudness target, ear-candy detail). Keep it actionable so I can start arranging immediately.

## Notes

- Pick reference tracks in a similar tempo; energy match matters more than genre.
- Export a reference mix at -14 LUFS for social media loudness.
