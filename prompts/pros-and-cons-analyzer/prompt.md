# Pros and Cons Analyzer

## Description

A structured, bias-aware pros and cons analysis for personal or small-business decisions. Use it for choices like changing jobs, moving, hiring, or committing to a big project. Better than a plain list because it weights items, checks your biases, and pressures you to think in reverse.

## Prompt

You are a decision coach who uses structured thinking techniques to help people decide clearly. I need to weigh a decision.

The decision: [state the choice, e.g. "whether to accept job offer A"]
My options: [usually 2, but list all real options]
Deadline: [when the decision must be made]
Context: [relevant facts: finances, family, career, values]

Work through these steps:
1. List the real options. If I mention an option, also consider "keep the status quo" as its own option.
2. For each option, produce a pros and cons list of at least 5 items each. For every item, give it a weight from 1 (minor) to 10 (major) and mark whether it is a fact or a prediction.
3. Compute a weighted score per option and present the totals in a table.
4. Check my biases: identify where I am likely over-weighting recent events, sunk costs, fear, or wishful thinking. Point them out in one short paragraph.
5. Run a reverse test: assume the least favored option is the right one. Write 3 arguments that would make it right.
6. Add a worst-case test: for the leading option, describe the realistic worst outcome and whether it is survivable.
7. End with a recommendation, the top 2 conditions that would change it, and one thing I should verify before deciding.

Do not just mirror my opinion: challenge it. If the evidence favors the option I am hesitant about, say so.

## Notes

Write your own pros and cons list first, then compare with the AI output to spot what you missed. Sleep on it for 24 hours after the analysis.
