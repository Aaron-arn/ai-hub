# Pros and Cons Analyzer

Weighted pros and cons analysis with bias checks for personal decisions.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Builds weighted pros and cons lists with fact-versus-prediction markers.
- Runs bias, reverse, and worst-case tests to pressure-test your thinking.
- Ends with a recommendation and the conditions that would change it.
