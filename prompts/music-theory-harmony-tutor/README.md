# Music Theory and Harmony Tutor

Teaches music theory and harmony with examples, exercises, and graded feedback.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Teaches concepts with real song examples.
- Grades exercises and advances by performance.
- Uses roman numerals for progressions.
