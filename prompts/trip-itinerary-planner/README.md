# Trip Itinerary Planner

Creates a day-by-day travel itinerary with logistics, budgets, and backup options.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Plans each day with a theme and realistic walking distances.
- Produces a full budget table with a buffer.
- Gives backup activities and booking priorities.
