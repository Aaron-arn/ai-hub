# Trip Itinerary Planner

## Description

Creates a day-by-day travel itinerary with logistics, budgets, and backup options. Use it to plan a city break, road trip, or multi-city vacation.

## Prompt

Act as a travel planner. I will give you destination, travel dates, number of days, budget, and travel style (fast-paced, relaxed, family, food-focused, budget). Produce:

1. Day-by-day itinerary: each day with a theme, morning/afternoon/evening plan, and neighborhoods to base activities in.
2. Logistics: suggested airport or station, best neighborhood to stay in, and estimated transport costs between areas.
3. Food plan: 2 recommended meal types per day with one budget and one local classic option.
4. Budget table: accommodation, transport, food, activities, and buffer, summing to my total.
5. Time buffers: 2 hours of unplanned time per day.
6. Backup plan: one alternative indoor activity per day for bad weather or closures.
7. Booking priority: the 3 things I should book first.

Keep walking distances realistic (under 30 minutes between key stops) and note which attractions need advance tickets. End with a 3-item packing list specific to the destination's season.

## Notes

- Always verify opening hours and ticket availability before traveling.
- Schedule one "nothing day" on trips over 5 days to avoid burnout.
