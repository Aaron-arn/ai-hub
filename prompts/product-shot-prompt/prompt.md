# Product Shot

## Description

Studio-quality product photography prompt with lighting recipes.

## Prompt

Photograph {PRODUCT} as a premium studio product shot.

Style: {STYLE} (e.g., minimal light gray background, color splash, natural lifestyle)
Lighting recipe: {LIGHTING} - e.g., softbox key light 45 degrees, silver reflector fill, rim light from behind
Camera: 85mm lens, f/8, focus stack sharp from front to back
Props: {PROPS} (e.g., marble pedestal, green leaves, coffee beans)
Background: {BACKGROUND} - seamless paper, subtle gradient

Details: realistic reflections, gentle soft shadow under the product, subtle dust particles in the light. 1:1 crop, high-key or low-key per mood.
