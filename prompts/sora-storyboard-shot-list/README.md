# Sora Storyboard and Shot List

Turns a video idea into a shot-by-shot storyboard with ready-to-paste AI generation prompts.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Splits a concept into 8-12 timed shots with camera moves.
- Gives a paste-ready generation prompt per shot for Sora, Veo, or Runway.
- Ends with a continuity and consistency checklist.
