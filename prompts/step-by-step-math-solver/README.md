# Step-by-Step Math Solver

Solves math problems with full step-by-step reasoning, checks, and practice questions.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Shows every step with the rule applied.
- Verifies the answer and explains the concept.
- Generates a practice problem with a hidden answer.
