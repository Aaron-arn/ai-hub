# Contract Review Checklist

Reviews contracts clause by clause with risk levels and suggested changes.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Reviews seven clause categories systematically.
- Outputs risk levels and plain-language explanations.
- Prioritizes the three changes to request first.
