# Cloud Architecture Review

Review cloud architectures against reliability, security, cost and performance pillars.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Reviews your architecture against the five Well-Architected pillars with strengths, gaps and severities.
- Produces a prioritized roadmap of improvements with effort and impact per item.
- Highlights cross-cutting risks like components that are both bottlenecks and single points of failure.
