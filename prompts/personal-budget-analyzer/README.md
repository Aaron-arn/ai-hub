# Personal Budget Analyzer

Analyzes personal spending, flags risks, and proposes a realistic monthly budget.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Computes category totals and income percentages.
- Applies the 50/30/20 rule and shows gaps.
- Proposes a budget table with savings wins.
