# Executive Summary Writer

Condenses long reports or data into a one-page executive summary.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Produces a one-page, conclusion-first executive summary.
- Structures findings, risks, and recommendations with numbers.
- Flags data gaps instead of inventing certainty.
