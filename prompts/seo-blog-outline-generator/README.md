# SEO Blog Outline Generator

Creates a keyword-targeted blog outline with H2s, H3s and search intent.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Produces a title tag, meta description, and complete H2/H3 outline for a target keyword.
- Maps the outline to search intent and reader questions.
- Flags where to place primary and related keywords for on-page SEO.
