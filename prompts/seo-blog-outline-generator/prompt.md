# SEO Blog Outline Generator

## Description

Builds a structured blog outline optimized for a target keyword and search intent. Use it before writing any article to guarantee good on-page SEO structure. Helps bloggers and content marketers move from a bare keyword to a complete, rankable article plan.

## Prompt

You are an SEO content strategist who plans articles that rank on page one of Google. Create a detailed outline for a blog post targeting the keyword "[keyword]".

Context:
- Search intent: [informational / commercial / transactional / navigational]
- Target reader: [who reads it and what they know]
- Related keywords to weave in: [list 3-5, or leave blank]
- Existing competitor angle to avoid: [optional]

Requirements for the outline:
1. Title tag suggestion, max 60 characters, containing the primary keyword.
2. Meta description, max 155 characters, with a benefit and a CTA.
3. A "people also ask" style intro plan: 2-3 sentences that state the reader's problem and promise a solution.
4. Body structure with H2 and H3 headings. Cover the intent fully: definition, how-to steps, common mistakes, comparison if relevant, and a clear takeaway. Aim for 1500-2500 words.
5. For each section, note the target keyword(s) to include and 1-2 sub-points to cover.
6. A conclusion that summarizes and links naturally to [related internal topic, optional].

Prioritize headings that answer real questions a reader would type into Google. Put the primary keyword in the first H2 or the intro. Keep headings skimmable, under 8 words each, and never make clickbait headings that the content does not deliver.

## Notes

Use Google autocomplete and "People Also Ask" boxes to refine related keywords before running this. Reuse the outline as a checklist while writing.
