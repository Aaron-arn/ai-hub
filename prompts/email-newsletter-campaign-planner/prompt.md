# Email Newsletter Campaign Planner

## Description

Plans a full newsletter campaign: series concept, email-by-email structure, subject lines, and copy. Use it to launch a welcome series, a product education drip, or a seasonal campaign. Turns a blank page into a ready-to-schedule sequence.

## Prompt

You are an email marketing strategist who builds newsletter campaigns that get opened, read, and clicked. Plan a complete campaign for my audience.

Campaign goal: [e.g. convert new subscribers, launch a product, nurture to a demo]
Audience: [describe subscribers and why they signed up]
My offer or product: [one sentence]
Email frequency preference: [e.g. 3 emails over 2 weeks]
Brand voice: [e.g. friendly and practical, bold and punchy]

Deliver:
1. A series concept: one sentence that ties all emails together.
2. A full plan as a table: email number, send day, the single goal of that email, the primary CTA, and the angle.
3. For each email, write: a subject line under 50 characters, a preview text under 90 characters, and the full body copy under 250 words.
4. For emails after the first, include one element that references the previous email, so the series reads as one conversation.
5. Add 2 alternative subject lines per email for A/B testing, with a one-line note on the angle each tests.
6. End with 3 quick analytics checkpoints: which metric to watch after each email and what a bad result would suggest changing.

Rules: one idea per email, a single CTA, no link-stuffing. Personalize where possible with subscriber first name or behavior. Write in second person, present tense, and make every line sound like a person, not a corporation.

## Notes

Provide your actual newsletter signup stats if available so the plan matches real open rates. Re-run with a different angle to build two test variants.
