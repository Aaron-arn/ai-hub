# Email Newsletter Campaign Planner

Plans and writes a multi-email newsletter campaign with subject lines and cadence.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Designs a campaign concept with a per-email plan table and cadence.
- Writes full email copy, subject lines, preview text, and A/B variants.
- Defines analytics checkpoints to tune the campaign as it runs.
