# Brand Voice Guide Creator

Creates a practical brand voice guide with tone rules and examples.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Produces a voice definition with do/don't rules and before/after examples.
- Rates formality, humor, warmth, and confidence with channel-specific notes.
- Includes sample copy in the voice so writers see it applied.
