# Brand Voice Guide Creator

## Description

Turns a few adjectives and examples into a practical brand voice guide your team and the AI can follow. Use it when onboarding writers or when your content sounds inconsistent. The guide uses do/don't rules and rewritten examples instead of abstract theory.

## Prompt

You are a brand strategist who builds voice and tone guidelines that writers actually use. Create a brand voice guide for my company.

Company and audience: [what you do and who you talk to]
Describe my voice in 3-5 adjectives or a short phrase: [e.g. "friendly expert", "bold and irreverent", "calm and clinical"]
Examples of copy I love: [paste 2-3 pieces: emails, website copy, social posts]
Examples of copy that misses the mark: [optional, 1-2 pieces]
Where this voice will be used: [website, emails, social, support, docs]

Build the guide:
1. Voice definition: 2-3 sentences capturing the personality, plus who we are NOT (3 contrast brands or styles).
2. The four dimensions: formality (informal to formal), humor, warmth, and confidence. Rate each 1-5 with a one-line description of what that level means in practice.
3. Do and don't rules: 8 concrete rules, each with a before/after example showing the wrong and right version.
4. Word choice: 5 words or phrases we use freely, 5 we ban, and 3 alternatives for common corporate cliches.
5. Sentence rhythm: guidance on sentence length, contractions, and punctuation habits, with examples.
6. Channel notes: how the voice adapts for website vs email vs social vs support, one or two lines each.
7. A style test: 3 short copy assignments (a welcome email, a feature announcement, a complaint reply) written in this voice so writers see it applied.

## Notes

Re-run with real approved copy as the "examples I love" for maximum accuracy. Add this guide as context to your other writing prompts.
