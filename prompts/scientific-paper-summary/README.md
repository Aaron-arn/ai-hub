# Scientific Paper Summary

Summarizes scientific papers with question, method, results, limitations, and implications.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Condenses a paper into seven clear sections.
- Flags missing data as "not reported".
- Ends with reviewer questions and implications.
