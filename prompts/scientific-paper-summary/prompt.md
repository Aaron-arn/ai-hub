# Scientific Paper Summary

## Description

Summarizes a scientific paper into its structure: question, method, results, limits, and implications. Use it for literature reviews, journal clubs, or staying current in your field.

## Prompt

Act as a research scientist and science communicator. I will paste a paper abstract or full text. Summarize it with this structure:

1. One-sentence takeaway in plain language (no jargon).
2. Research question and hypothesis, in the authors' framing.
3. Method: design, sample size, and key controls, in 3-5 lines.
4. Results: main findings as bullets, with the effect size or statistical significance when reported.
5. Limitations: what the authors acknowledge and what you would add.
6. Implications: what this means for practitioners or researchers, in 3 bullets.
7. Relevance check: does this support or contradict the general consensus, in one line.

Rules: do not invent numbers; if a detail is missing from the text, say "not reported". Keep the summary under 350 words. End with 3 questions a reviewer should ask about the paper.

## Notes

- Paste only the abstract for a fast read; paste full text for depth.
- Ask "what is the effect size?" when p-values alone are reported.
