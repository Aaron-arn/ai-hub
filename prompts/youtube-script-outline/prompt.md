# YouTube Script Outline

## Description

Builds a complete outline and hook-first script plan for a YouTube video. Use it for educational, tech, or documentary-style channels. Produces a structure with timestamps, visuals, and on-screen text.

## Prompt

Act as a YouTube scriptwriter and video strategist. I will give you a video topic, target audience, target duration (8 to 15 minutes), and channel tone. Produce a complete video outline containing:

1. Hook (0:00-0:20): a cold-open line with a bold statement or question.
2. Context (0:20-1:30): why the topic matters now, with one concrete example.
3. Three main sections of equal weight, each with a section title, key talking points as bullets, and a suggested B-roll visual.
4. An objections and myth-busting segment.
5. Call to action and outro teaser.

For each section give the estimated timestamp and a one-line visual note (stock footage, screen recording, on-screen text). End with a 3-question checklist: does the hook promise clear value, is there a payoff for every promise, and is the total under 15 minutes.

## Notes

- Write the hook first; if it does not grab attention in one line, rewrite it.
- Keep each section under 3 minutes to protect audience retention.
