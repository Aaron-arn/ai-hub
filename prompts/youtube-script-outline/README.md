# YouTube Script Outline

Builds a complete YouTube video outline with hooks, sections, timestamps, and visual notes.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Produces a hook-first outline with timestamps.
- Breaks the video into three balanced sections with visuals.
- Includes an objections segment and call to action.
