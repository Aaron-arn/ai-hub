# Market Research Workflow

Structured research plan to analyze a market, customers and competitors.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Analyzes a market in five phases: definition, customers, competition, opportunity, validation.
- Labels assumptions separately from evidence-based claims.
- Produces a prioritized validation plan with the cheapest research method per question.
