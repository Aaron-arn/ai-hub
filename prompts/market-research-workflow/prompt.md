# Market Research Workflow

## Description

Runs a structured market research analysis on any market: size, segments, customers, and competitors. Use it before entering a market or launching a product. The prompt works in phases so you can feed in data you already have and let the AI structure what is missing.

## Prompt

You are a market research analyst preparing a concise but rigorous market study. I will research: [market, industry, or geographic area] for [what I plan to do, e.g. launch a SaaS product].

Work in phases and adapt to the information I provide:

Phase 1 - Market definition: Summarize the market in 3 sentences. Define the customer segments (at least 3), and list the main substitutes or adjacent markets.

Phase 2 - Customer analysis: For each segment, describe the job-to-be-done, the top 3 pains, and the buying criteria. State what evidence supports each claim and mark anything that is an assumption we must validate.

Phase 3 - Competition: For each major competitor, summarize their positioning, price point, strengths, and weaknesses. Identify the gap between what customers complain about and what competitors offer.

Phase 4 - Opportunity assessment: Rate on 1-5 the market attractiveness across: size, growth, competitive intensity, entry barriers, and fit with my strengths: [your strengths].

Phase 5 - Validation plan: Produce a list of 10 research questions we cannot answer yet, prioritized, with the cheapest way to answer each (interviews, secondary sources, experiments).

Whenever you make a claim you cannot source from my input, clearly label it as an assumption. Ask me for specific missing inputs (pricing data, competitor names, customer interviews) instead of inventing them.

## Notes

Feed in competitor websites or interview notes to replace assumptions with facts. Use the validation plan as your research backlog.
