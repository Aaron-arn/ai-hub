# Cinematic Product Shot

Generates a cinematic product photography prompt ready to paste into Midjourney, DALL-E, or Stable Diffusion.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Builds a complete generation prompt from product, mood, and audience details.
- Includes lighting, camera, and style keywords plus a negative prompt.
- Offers two ready variations for different moods.
