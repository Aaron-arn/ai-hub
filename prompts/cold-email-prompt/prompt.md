# Cold Email

## Description

Write personalized outreach emails that get replies.

## Prompt

Write a cold outreach email to {RECIPIENT} (role: {ROLE}, company: {COMPANY}).

Context: {CONTEXT} (what you want, why you're emailing them specifically)
Offer: {OFFER}

Structure (email under 120 words):
1. Subject line (max 8 words, curiosity or specificity, no spam words)
2. Personalization opener: one line proving you looked at their work specifically
3. Value statement: how you help people like them, with a concrete result
4. Soft ask: one small low-commitment request (reply to a question, 15-min call)
5. Sign-off

Rules: never start with "I hope", no "I wanted to reach out", no attachment, single CTA. Provide the email plus 3 alternative subject lines.
