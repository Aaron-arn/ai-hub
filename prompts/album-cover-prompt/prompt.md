# Album Cover

## Description

Design a striking album cover with typography and strong visual metaphor.

## Prompt

Design an album cover (square 1:1) for {ARTIST} titled "{ALBUM_TITLE}".

Genre: {GENRE} (e.g., ambient, techno, indie folk)
Concept: {CONCEPT} - a single strong visual metaphor of the album's mood
Composition: main image fills 80% of the frame; album title set in {TYPOGRAPHY} across the top; artist name small at the bottom.
Texture: vinyl-grain film grain or matte paper texture overlay
Palette: {PALETTE}

Rules: no extra text, no faces if not specified, strong contrast, memorable at thumbnail size, subtle glow or shadow to unify.
