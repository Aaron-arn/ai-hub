# Lesson Plan Creator

## Description

Designs a complete, time-boxed lesson plan with learning objectives, activities, and assessment. Use it for tutoring sessions, workshops, or classroom teaching. The plan follows the objective-activity-assessment loop so every minute of the lesson serves the goal.

## Prompt

You are an instructional designer who builds lessons that keep learners engaged and actually retain the material. Design a complete lesson plan.

Topic: [what will be taught]
Learners: [age, level, group size, prior knowledge]
Duration: [e.g. 60 minutes]
Format: [in person / online / self-paced]
Materials available: [e.g. whiteboard, projector, no tech, printed handouts]
Learning goal: [what they should know or do by the end, or let me define it]

Build the plan:
1. Learning objectives: 2-4 objectives in the format "By the end of this lesson, learners will be able to [verb] [measurable outcome]". Use action verbs like explain, apply, compare, build, not "understand".
2. A time-boxed plan table: phase (hook, instruction, practice, application, assessment), minutes, activity, and what the instructor does vs what learners do.
3. The hook: a 5-minute opening that connects the topic to something the learners already care about, with 2 alternative hooks.
4. Practice activities: 2-3 hands-on activities that increase in difficulty. For each, state the materials, instructions in 3 steps, and the common mistakes to watch for.
5. Differentiation: how to adapt the lesson for faster learners, slower learners, and one learner with a specific challenge: [optional].
6. Assessment: a 5-minute exit check (3 questions or one short task) that measures the objectives, plus the pass threshold.
7. Homework or follow-up: one optional task for learners who want more.

Rules: every activity must map to at least one objective. Keep instructions to learners under 3 sentences. Prefer activities where learners do something over slides. If I have not given the learning goal, choose one and state it clearly at the top.

## Notes

Reuse the plan skeleton for the same group on other topics. Ask for a worksheet version with answer keys for the practice activities.
