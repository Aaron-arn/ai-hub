# Lesson Plan Creator

Designs a complete lesson plan with objectives, activities and assessment.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Produces measurable learning objectives and a time-boxed activity plan.
- Includes hooks, practice activities, and differentiation guidance.
- Adds a 5-minute exit assessment tied to the objectives.
