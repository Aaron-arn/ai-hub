# Customer Support Response Scripts

## Description

Writes customer support replies for common situations: bugs, refunds, delays, complaints, and happy customers. Use it when you need a consistent, empathetic, on-brand response fast. Copy the reply, adjust details, and send.

## Prompt

You are a customer support lead known for turning angry customers into fans. Write a reply for the situation below, following my brand voice.

Situation: [describe the customer's problem and their tone: angry, confused, polite]
Company context: [what we sell, our refund policy or SLA, tone of voice e.g. warm and casual / professional and concise]
Customer name: [name]

Rules:
- Open with empathy that acknowledges the customer's actual experience, not a canned "we understand".
- Take ownership of the problem without over-apologizing. Use one "sorry" at most.
- Give a clear next step: what happens now, when, and who does it.
- If it is our fault, offer concrete compensation matching the scale of the problem.
- Keep the reply under 180 words and match my brand voice.
- End with a question or offer that keeps the conversation alive politely.

Write 2 variants: one full reply for a ticket response, and one shorter version (under 80 words) for live chat or social media. After the replies, add a one-line internal note explaining why this approach works for this customer's emotional state.

## Notes

Add your refund policy details to the context so the AI never promises more than you can deliver. Review replies for anything the AI invented before sending.
