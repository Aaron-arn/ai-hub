# Customer Support Response Scripts

Drafts empathetic, on-brand replies to common customer support situations.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Generates a full ticket reply and a short chat/social variant for the same situation.
- Enforces empathy-first structure, limited apologies, and a clear next step.
- Includes a rationale note so your team learns why the reply works.
