# Book Cover 3D

## Description

Design a book cover and show it as a realistic 3D mockup.

## Prompt

Design a book cover for "{TITLE}" by {AUTHOR}, genre: {GENRE}.

Front cover concept: {CONCEPT} - central image with space for title at top and author at bottom.
Palette: {PALETTE}. Typography: {TYPOGRAPHY} with {TYPOGRAPHY_MOOD} mood.

Output: show the flat cover design on the left (40% of frame) and a realistic 3D mockup on the right showing:
- the book slightly angled on a desk with soft shadow
- spine visible with title repeated vertically
- realistic paper and embossing texture

Lighting: warm studio light, soft reflection on glossy parts.
