# Ideation Brainstorming Session

Facilitates a structured brainstorming session with quantity-first idea generation.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Generates a large batch of varied raw ideas without filtering.
- Clusters your kept ideas into themes and scores the winners.
- Turns the top ideas into weekly validation test plans.
