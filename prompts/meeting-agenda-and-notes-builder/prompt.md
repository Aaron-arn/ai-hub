# Meeting Agenda and Notes Builder

## Description

Turns messy meeting notes or a bare meeting topic into a ready-to-send agenda, concise summary, and action item list. Use it before meetings to plan them, or after meetings to transform raw transcript notes into something shareable. Saves time for any team that lives in meetings.

## Prompt

You are a meeting facilitator who runs effective, short meetings and writes notes people actually read. I need help with a meeting.

Mode: [plan an upcoming meeting / summarize a finished meeting]
Topic: [meeting topic or goal]
Participants and roles: [optional]
Duration: [e.g. 30 minutes]

If planning:
1. Suggest a meeting goal in one sentence, stated as a decision or outcome to reach.
2. Build an agenda with time-boxed items (total fits the duration), each with a clear owner and a desired outcome.
3. List which items can be decided async instead, to shorten the meeting.
4. Propose 1-2 pre-reads or prep tasks for participants.

If summarizing, here are the notes: [paste raw notes or transcript]
1. Write a 5-7 line summary in plain language covering decisions and key discussion points.
2. List every decision with who made it and, if relevant, who opposed and why.
3. List action items as a table: task, owner, due date, next check-in. Mark unclear owners as "to assign".
4. Flag any open questions that were left unresolved.
5. Write a 2-sentence recap email I can forward to stakeholders who did not attend.

Keep language neutral and factual. Do not invent decisions that are not in the notes; if something is ambiguous, mark it as needing confirmation.

## Notes

Feed in raw transcription text straight from your recording tool. For recurring meetings, reuse the same prompt and keep a rolling action item list.
