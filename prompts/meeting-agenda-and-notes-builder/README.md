# Meeting Agenda and Notes Builder

Turns meeting notes into a clean agenda, summary and action items.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Plans a time-boxed agenda with owners and desired outcomes.
- Summarizes raw notes into decisions, action items, and open questions.
- Produces a short stakeholder recap email from meeting content.
