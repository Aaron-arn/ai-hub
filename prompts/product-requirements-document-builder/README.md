# Product Requirements Document Builder

Writes a complete PRD with goals, user stories, specs and success metrics.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Produces a full PRD: goals, non-goals, personas, stories, and requirements.
- Writes testable acceptance criteria and edge-case coverage.
- Adds success metrics, guardrails, risks, and open questions.
