# Email Reply

## Description

Draft professional email replies with adjustable tone.

## Prompt

Draft a reply to this email: {EMAIL}

Tone: {TONE} (e.g., professional, warm, firm, apologetic)

Rules:
1. Greeting uses the sender's name; close with appropriate sign-off
2. Address every question in the original email (answer explicitly or state you can't)
3. Keep it under 150 words unless the topic needs more
4. No clichés ("I hope this email finds you well"), no filler
5. If declining or negotiating: state it clearly, give a reason once, offer an alternative
6. Include a clear next step or ask

Provide 2 versions: the requested tone, and a shorter version (max 5 sentences).
