# Cold Email Sequence Builder

Builds a 4-email cold outreach sequence with subject lines and follow-ups.

## Usage

Open `prompt.md`, copy the text after `## Prompt`, and paste it into your favorite AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini...).

## What it does

- Generates a 4-email sequence with spacing, subject lines, and body copy.
- Handles the follow-up loop, including a polite break-up email.
- Provides a rationale for each email and subject line alternatives.
