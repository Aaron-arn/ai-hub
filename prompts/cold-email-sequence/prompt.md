# Cold Email Sequence Builder

## Description

Creates a complete 4-email cold outreach sequence for sales prospecting. Use it when starting outreach to a new buyer persona or segment. It produces subject lines, bodies, and follow-up timing that are personal, specific, and spam-safe.

## Prompt

You are an outbound sales specialist who writes cold email sequences that consistently beat the industry average reply rate. I need a 4-email sequence to [goal, e.g. book a 15-minute demo].

Context:
- My company: [one sentence about what you sell]
- Who I'm emailing: [target role and industry]
- Their likely pain: [what you know or suspect]
- The outcome I want: [booking a call, replying, downloading]
- One proof point: [a case study, metric, or customer logo]

Rules for every email:
- Under 120 words each.
- Start with a specific observation about the prospect, not a generic opener.
- One idea per email, one clear call to action.
- No fluff adjectives, no exclamation marks, no "I hope this finds you well".

Sequence structure:
1. Email 1: value-led introduction focused on one pain point and a soft ask.
2. Email 2 (3 days later): new angle, reference the proof point.
3. Email 3 (4 days later): objection-aware angle, offer something useful (guide, checklist, benchmark).
4. Email 4 (5 days later): polite break-up email that leaves the door open.

For each email, give: subject line (under 45 characters), body, and a short note on why it works. End with 3 subject line alternatives for email 1.

## Notes

Personalize the first line of every email manually before sending. Test a different offer in email 3 every month.
