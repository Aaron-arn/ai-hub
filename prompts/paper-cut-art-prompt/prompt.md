# Paper Cut Art

## Description

Create layered paper-cut style artwork with depth and soft shadows.

## Prompt

Create a paper-cut style illustration of {SCENE}.

Layers (from back to front):
1. Sky/background layer: {SKY_COLOR} with a simple sun or moon cut-out
2. Mid-ground: {LANDSCAPE} (mountains, hills, city skyline) in 2-3 layered colors
3. Foreground: {SUBJECT} as a bold paper silhouette
4. Decorations: small cut-out shapes (leaves, birds, stars) floating

Style rules: flat matte colors, clean vector-like edges, every layer cast a soft drop shadow onto the layer below (3mm), subtle paper grain texture, pastel palette {PALETTE}. Composition centered, slight vertical tilt for depth.
